ATIC Event Check-In

Team project · Attendance system for a 2-day, 4-track workshop event

An admin tool that runs attendance for a multi-track event, from importing participants to emailing certificates.

  • Imports participants from Excel or CSV and generates a personal QR code for each one
  • Sends QR codes by email, or exports them all as a ZIP
  • In-browser QR scanning for the opening ceremony and each workshop slot, with on-time and late tracking
  • Live dashboard with per-track occupancy and per-student history
  • Calculates attendance rates and automatically emails certificates to eligible participants
